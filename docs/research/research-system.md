# Research and Progression

Source: [Research (Kingdom Adventurers)](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Research_(Kingdom_Adventurers)),
[Tips](https://kairosoft.fandom.com/wiki/Tips_(Kingdom_Adventurers)).

## Not a point-spending tree

The important structural fact: there is **no research currency**. Research
consumes **actual materials and sample items**, and availability is gated by
**Town Hall rank**. The wiki's own framing: raise your Town Hall rank and more
research becomes available.

So progression has two independent axes:

1. **Town Hall rank** — what is even offered to you
2. **Materials on hand** — whether you can afford it

## What research unlocks

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Facilities | Environment, Materials, Amenity, Indoor |
| Equipment | Weapons, helmets, armor, shields, accessories |
| Items | Consumable potions, orbs, special items |

## Cost currencies

Two kinds of ingredient:

**Crafted materials** — Strong Rope, Copper Coin, High Grade Brick, Sturdy Board,
Large Nail.

**Samples** — Weapon Sample, Helmet Sample, Shield Sample, Armor Sample,
Accessory Sample. These gate equipment research specifically.

**Knowledge items** — Sage's Tome, Scroll of Knowledge.

## Representative entries

| Unlock | TH rank | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Master's Sword (rank C) | 6 | Weapon Sample ×4+, Sage's Tome ×5+ |
| Defensive Wall | 26 | Sturdy Board ×36+, Copper Coin ×1185+ |

Note the scale difference: rank 6 research costs single-digit samples; rank 26
costs over a thousand copper coins' worth of ingredients. Costs escalate steeply
with Town Hall rank.

## Town Hall rank — the master progression track

Town Hall rank drives nearly everything:

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Research availability | Higher rank unlocks more entries |
| Territory radius | +2 tiles per 10 levels (base 14) |
| Monarch rank | Advances every 15 levels: D→C at 15, B at 30, A at 45, S at 60 |
| Monarch strength | The manual notes the ruler's rank rises with the town's |

## Surveys

Surveys are one of the three ways to obtain facilities (alongside research and
map rewards). Survey resident rank matters — a tip notes a **B+ rank Survey
resident** unlocks the first High-Grade Storage for zero Silver Coins.

## What this means for our build

- v1 gated buildings on population/renown; v2 gates them on **Town Hall rank +
  materials**, which is both more faithful and gives materials a purpose beyond
  construction.
- "Samples" are a neat idea: a distinct ingredient class whose only job is to
  gate a category of research. Worth keeping.
- The real game's gacha is one of the facility sources. We replace it with a
  **Surveyor's Office** spending materials — same role (a randomised source of
  new facility types), no monetisation.
