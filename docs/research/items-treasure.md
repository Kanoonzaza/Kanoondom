# Items and Treasure

Source: [Items (Kingdom Adventurers)](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Kingdom_Adventurers)),
[Manual transcript](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Transcript:Manual_(Kingdom_Adventurers)).

## Three item classes

| Class | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Training items** | Foods and materials that raise resident stats — HP, MP, ATK, DEF, SPD, INT, DEX, Gather, Move |
| **Pet development items** | Fed to eggs during incubation; carry Attack / Defense / Balance / Special / Experience properties |
| **Research materials** | Ingredients that unlock equipment, potions and facilities |

## Where items come from

The wiki's summary: items come easily from **deploying residents to explore
outside the town**; deploying to **survey** yields rarer items; and **cooking
sometimes produces them** too. Shops sell some for coin.

So the item economy is fed by three loops the player already runs — exploration,
surveying, and the restaurant/kitchen chain — rather than by a separate grind.

## Representative items

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Kairo Cookie | ~400 points spread across all stat categories — premium |
| Sweet Medicine | 100 points to every stat type |
| Energy Drink | 55 points across all training categories |

## Group items — the interesting ones

A separate class of powerful one-off items, typically from map rewards:

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Eternal Candle** | Grants a resident **infinite lifespan** |
| **Miracle Mallet** | **Instantly completes** a facility's construction |

The manual advises considering carefully when to use them — they are scarce
enough to be decisions rather than conveniences.

## Treasure

Treasure is its own resource with its own storage building, obtained largely from
caves and map exploration. The Treasure Room is one of the special buildings on
the main page.

## What this means for our build

- Items give **training a currency**: raising a resident's stats is a thing you
  spend on, not just something that happens on level-up.
- Pet development items are the steering mechanism for egg hatching — they are
  what makes the creature system a decision rather than a lottery
  (see [creature-collection.md](creature-collection.md)).
- Group items are a nice source of rare, high-impact choices. **Eternal Candle is
  moot for us** since we have no permadeath; we will repurpose or drop it.
- The three acquisition loops all already exist in our plan (exploration,
  surveys, restaurants), so items slot in without new subsystems.
